### 📖 The Story So Far

> *"I automate the repetitive, error-prone, and hard-to-reach work with data and AI."*

Whether it is a state highway division bogged down by six disconnected spreadsheets, educators manually hand-keying exam scores from stacks of paper, or urban lakes accumulating surface debris, I look for the point of maximum human friction. 

I am an AI & Machine Learning Engineering undergraduate at **Velalar College of Engineering and Technology** (Batch of 2027, CGPA 8.05/10). My instinct is simple: dissect the manual breakdown, architect a resilient pipeline using **Python and SQL**, and deliver software that makes operations verifiable, fast, and repeatable.
